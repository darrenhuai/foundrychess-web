ChessTan — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

ChessTan ("the game") is a small, independently developed project. This page describes what data the game processes and why, in plain language.

No accounts, no tracking

ChessTan does not require you to create an account, does not collect analytics, does not use advertising or tracking technology of any kind, and does not sell or share any data with third parties. Local hot-seat play and play against the built-in AI never send any data anywhere.

Online multiplayer

When you use the game's online multiplayer feature, your device connects to a relay server that forwards gameplay messages (dice rolls, moves, chat text you type) between the players in your match. This connection necessarily involves your device's IP address, the same way any online multiplayer game or website you visit does. The relay server does not store, log, or retain this traffic beyond what's needed to keep an active match connected — it is not read, analyzed, or shared for any other purpose.

If you play through Steam's own multiplayer option instead of the relay, that connection is handled by Valve's Steam networking, subject to Valve's own Privacy Policy.

In-game chat

Text you type into the in-game chat during an online match is sent only to the other player(s) in that match, the same way the rest of the match's gameplay data is — it is not stored after the match ends.

Local settings

Volume levels, display preferences, and similar settings are saved only to a local configuration file on your own device. This data never leaves your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change too. Continued use of the game after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
